Before we get started.

I'm not sure where AI would help. Is that okay?

Yes. Most conversations start there. We walk through how your work gets done, and I'll point out where AI or automation could help, and where it wouldn't be worth it.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Usually, yes. I build around the systems you already have and connect them where they allow it. If a tool can't be connected, I'll tell you early.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

Systems are tested on real scenarios before they go live, and anything uncertain is sent to a person instead of being acted on. You decide which steps always need a human check.

How are projects priced?

After our first conversation, I'll give you a clear proposal with the scope and the price, agreed before any build starts.

Do we need to be in Auckland?

No. I'm based in Auckland and happy to meet in person here, and I work with businesses elsewhere in New Zealand online.
