Start small. Prove the value. Build from there.

You do not need a fully formed AI strategy to get started. You need a problem worth solving and a clear next step.

  1. Understand your business

    Walk through the work, the tools and the bottlenecks. Agree on the problem and what a useful result would look like.

  2. Choose a focused first project

    Define a manageable scope, the data required, and the checkpoints. Agree on the proposal before the build begins.

  3. Build, test and put it to work

    Develop the solution around your workflow. Test real scenarios, review the results together, and prepare your team to use it.

  4. Review and refine

    See what is working, resolve the rough edges, and decide whether to improve the first workflow or tackle the next one.
