Services / 1. Discover

Find your best AI opportunity.

Before anything is built, we work out where AI could actually help, and what is worth doing first.

How it works

  1. Walk through the work

    We look at how the work is really done today: the tasks, the tools, the hand-offs and where time goes.

  2. Check what is feasible

    For each idea, I look at the data you have, the tools involved, the cost and the risk. Sometimes the best answer is a simple automation or a process change, not AI.

  3. Agree a first project

    You get a short, prioritised plan. The first project is scoped tightly enough to show value quickly.

Decisions backed by data.

With a background in data analytics, I look for opportunities you can measure. Each idea in the plan comes with what it would change and how we would know it worked.

What you get

  • Workflow and opportunity review
  • Feasibility, tools and data assessment
  • A prioritised implementation plan

A good fit if

  • You know where the time goes, but not whether AI would help.
  • You have tried tools like ChatGPT and want to go further.
  • You want an independent view before paying for new software.
