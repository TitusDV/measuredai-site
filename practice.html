Less chasing. More getting things done.

A useful system starts with a familiar problem. Explore a few ways AI and automation can fit into everyday work.

An order comes in. The follow-up takes care of itself.

Stock checks, order updates and shipping notifications can mean a lot of switching between systems. Connect those steps so your team can focus on the exceptions.

The opportunity
Less re-keying and chasing. A clearer path from order to dispatch.
Where people fit
Your team reviews unusual orders, stock conflicts and anything that needs a judgement call.
Discuss a workflow like this
  1. Receive the orderYour online store
  2. Check stock and order detailsConnected inventory systems
  3. Flag anything unusualA person reviews exceptions
  4. Update systems and notifyOrder records and customer updates

A question arrives. A good reply is ready to check.

Many customer emails ask the same few things. Look up the facts in your systems and draft the reply, so your team spends its time on the conversations that need them.

The opportunity
Faster, more consistent replies, without starting each one from scratch.
Where people fit
Your team approves replies, and handles complaints and anything sensitive.
Discuss a workflow like this
  1. Receive the enquiryEmail or your helpdesk
  2. Find the factsOrders, accounts and policies
  3. Draft a replyIn your tone, with the details filled in
  4. Review and sendA person approves or edits

Someone has a question. The answer is already written down.

Policies, procedures and product details often live in documents nobody has time to search. Let your team ask in plain language and get an answer that points to the source.

The opportunity
Less time hunting for information, and fewer questions for the one person who knows.
Where people fit
Answers show their sources, and say so when the documents don't cover a question.
Discuss a workflow like this
  1. Ask a questionIn plain language
  2. Search your documentsPolicies, guides and records
  3. Answer with sourcesLinked to the page it came from
  4. Check when it mattersA person confirms important answers
